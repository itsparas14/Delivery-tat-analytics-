# Business Requirements

## Business Problem

The Supply Chain Operations team required a centralized dashboard to
monitor shipment performance across couriers, warehouses, channels, and
delivery zones.

## Objectives

-   Track dispatched and delivered shipments.
-   Measure Dispatch TAT and Delivery TAT.
-   Monitor Delivery % and Fill Rate.
-   Compare courier performance.
-   Analyze channel and warehouse performance.
-   Identify operational bottlenecks.

## Users

-   Supply Chain Team
-   Warehouse Managers
-   Operations Leadership
